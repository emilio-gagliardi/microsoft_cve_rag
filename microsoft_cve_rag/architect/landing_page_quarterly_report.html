Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our vulnerability reports

What's the difference between your free advisories and these reports?

expand_more

Our free advisories provide immediate alerts about critical vulnerabilities with basic mitigation guidance. The quarterly reports offer comprehensive analysis, including vulnerability trends, patch effectiveness metrics, exploit development timelines, and detailed configuration recommendations that aren't available in our free content.

How current is the data in each report?

expand_more

Each quarterly report analyzes all Microsoft vulnerabilities disclosed during that quarter, with data updated through the final Patch Tuesday of the quarter. We include analysis of patch effectiveness and any newly discovered exploits up to 5 business days before report publication.

Can I share the report with my team?

expand_more

Yes! All subscriptions include unlimited sharing within your organization. The annual and enterprise plans allow you to share with contractors and MSSPs working on your behalf. We only ask that you don't publicly redistribute the reports.

Do you cover zero-day vulnerabilities?

expand_more

Absolutely. Each report includes a dedicated section analyzing any zero-day vulnerabilities disclosed during the quarter, including their exploitation patterns, industry impact, and long-term mitigation strategies beyond the initial patch.

What if I need help implementing your recommendations?

expand_more

Annual and enterprise subscribers receive priority support from our security analysts. We offer optional consulting services to help implement complex mitigation strategies, with discounts available for subscribers.
